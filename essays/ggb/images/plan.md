# Image search plan — Golden Gate Bridge

Research doc: `essays/ggb/research.md` — a full two-part essay (infrastructure project +
tourist icon). Nine visual themes, one subagent each.

Sources are Wikimedia Commons categories / Wikipedia article images first (the subject is
extremely well covered by 1930s public-domain federal and District photography), Flickr for
anything after ~2010, Internet Archive for maps and ephemera.

---

## 1. `ferries` — Before the bridge
The strait as a boat crossing: SF Ferry Building crowds, Sausalito auto ferries, Southern
Pacific–Golden Gate Ferries, the undeveloped Golden Gate strait.

- `Category:Ferry Building (San Francisco)` — 1920s exteriors and the crowded nave
- `Category:Ferries of San Francisco Bay` / "Sausalito ferry 1920s"
- Article `San Francisco Ferry Building`, article `Golden Gate (strait)`
- Commons search: "Golden Gate strait before bridge", "Southern Pacific Golden Gate Ferries"
- Internet Archive: San Francisco Bay ferry route maps, 1920s

## 2. `opposition-money` — Politics, the bond, the Depression
Southern Pacific and the ferry monopoly, the War Department and Fort Point, the November
1930 bond vote, A. P. Giannini and Bank of America, Depression-era San Francisco.

- Article `Amadeo Giannini`; `Category:Amadeo Giannini`
- `Category:Bank of America` (1930s SF headquarters)
- Commons search: "Golden Gate Bridge bond 1930", "San Francisco Great Depression 1932"
- `Category:Fort Point (San Francisco)` — the War Department land
- Internet Archive: Golden Gate Bridge and Highway District bond prospectus / campaign ephemera

## 3. `people` — The men credited and uncredited
Strauss, Charles Ellis, Leon Moisseiff, Irving Morrow, and the Strauss statue at the SF end.

- Article `Joseph Strauss (engineer)`; `Category:Joseph Strauss`
- Article `Charles Alton Ellis`; article `Leon Moisseiff`; article `Irving Morrow`
- `Category:Statue of Joseph Strauss` / Commons search "Strauss statue Golden Gate Bridge"
- Commons search: "Ellis recognition plaque Golden Gate Bridge south tower"

## 4. `design` — Drawings, the rejected design, Art Deco, International Orange
Strauss's 1921 hybrid cantilever-suspension "rat-trap", the final suspension scheme,
engineering drawings and elevations, Morrow's Art Deco tower forms, railings, streetlamps,
and the colour.

- `Category:Drawings of the Golden Gate Bridge` / "Golden Gate Bridge blueprints"
- Commons search: "Strauss original Golden Gate Bridge design 1921 cantilever"
- Article `Golden Gate Bridge` (generator=images picks up the diagrams and elevations)
- `Category:International Orange` / Commons search "Golden Gate Bridge Art Deco detail"
- Internet Archive: Chief Engineer's Report plates, Morrow "Report on Color and Lighting" 1935

## 5. `construction` — 1933–1937
Caisson and pier work, tower erection, catwalks, cable spinning, deck hanging, aerials
showing the towers without a deck.

- `Category:Construction of the Golden Gate Bridge` — the primary well
- `Category:Golden Gate Bridge in 1935`, `…in 1936`, `…in 1937` if present
- Commons search: "Golden Gate Bridge cable spinning 1936", "Golden Gate Bridge south tower
  caisson 1934", "Golden Gate Bridge catwalk"
- Internet Archive: HAER / Chief Engineer's Report construction plates

## 6. `labor-safety` — Workers, the net, the fall
Ironworkers on high steel, riveting gangs, the safety net slung under the deck, Al Zampa
and the Halfway to Hell Club, the February 1937 scaffold collapse.

- `Category:Construction of the Golden Gate Bridge` (worker-focused files)
- Commons search: "Golden Gate Bridge workers 1936", "Golden Gate Bridge safety net",
  "Golden Gate Bridge ironworkers riveters"
- Article `Al Zampa`; `Category:Alfred Zampa Memorial Bridge` (portraits)
- Flickr `is_commons=1`: SF Public Library / Bancroft archive worker photographs

## 7. `opening-day` — May 1937
Pedestrian Day crowds, the 200,000 walkers, stunts and "firsts", Fiesta Week, FDR's
telegraph key, the 500-plane flyover and 42-ship fleet, the first cars and toll turnstiles.

- `Category:Opening of the Golden Gate Bridge` / Commons search "Golden Gate Bridge opening
  1937 pedestrian day"
- Commons search: "Golden Gate Bridge Fiesta 1937", "Golden Gate Bridge first cars 1937"
- Article `Golden Gate Bridge` images (opening-day photographs are in the article set)
- Internet Archive: 1937 opening programme, Fiesta Week ephemera, commemorative posters

## 8. `icon-views` — The bridge as a photograph
The canonical viewpoints the essay's second half depends on: Battery Spencer looking down
at the towers and skyline, Fort Point's arch framing the span, fog, aerials, sunset on the
International Orange, Baker Beach, Crissy Field, Marin Headlands.

- `Category:Golden Gate Bridge` and its view subcategories (`…from Battery Spencer`,
  `…in fog`, `…at sunset`, `…from Marin Headlands`)
- Commons search: "Fort Point Golden Gate Bridge arch", "Golden Gate Bridge fog Karl"
- Flickr (drop `is_commons`): recent high-quality Battery Spencer / Hawk Hill / Kirby Cove
- Also wanted: film-still-adjacent iconography if any is free (Vertigo's Fort Point location)

## 9. `modern` — The bridge now
The suicide-deterrent net (completed Jan 2024), the Phase 3B seismic retrofit, painters at
work, the cashless toll plaza, the west sidewalk railing and its 12,000 slats ("singing
bridge"), tourist crowds and the Welcome Center.

- Flickr (no `is_commons`): "Golden Gate Bridge suicide net 2024", "Golden Gate Bridge
  painters", "Golden Gate Bridge toll plaza", "Golden Gate Bridge railing sidewalk"
- Commons search: "Golden Gate Bridge suicide deterrent net", "Golden Gate Bridge toll plaza",
  "Golden Gate Bridge Welcome Center", "Golden Gate Bridge maintenance painting"
- `Category:Golden Gate Bridge` recent files; `Category:Loma Prieta earthquake` for the
  retrofit's motivating event

---

**Expected thin themes** (flagged in advance for the authoring pass): `opposition-money`
(bond campaigns and boardrooms are rarely photographed), `design` (drawings may be
copyright-restricted or absent from Commons), and `modern` (the retrofit had barely started
at the time of writing — Jan 2026 — so construction imagery may not exist yet).
